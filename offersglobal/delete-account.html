Account & Data Deletion — OffersGlobal AI

Step-by-step account deletion instructions · Publisher: AyaDigital LLC · App: com.offersglobal.app

1. Overview & Right to Erasure

At AyaDigital LLC, we uphold your absolute right to personal privacy, self-determination, and complete data erasure. Users and sellers on OffersGlobal AI (عروض جلوبال) can delete their user account, authentication profile, and all associated published offers at any time.

⚠️ What Happens When You Delete Your OffersGlobal AI Account?

  • Profile Purge: Your name, email address, password hash, and preferences are permanently wiped from production databases.
  • Published Deals Removal: All offers, discounts, and store listings published under your profile are permanently scrubbed and de-indexed from AI search.
  • Bookmarks & Saved Searches: Saved deals, bookmarked offers, and search preferences are permanently removed.

2. Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the OffersGlobal AI app installed on your smartphone and can sign in:

  1. Open the OffersGlobal AI application on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap the Account (حسابي) icon located in the top header.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section at the bottom of the page.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Delete my account / حذف الحساب نهائياً".
  5. Confirm the prompt to immediately terminate your account and erase all associated data.
