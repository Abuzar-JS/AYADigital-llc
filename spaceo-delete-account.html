Account and Data Deletion Request — spaceO (سبيس أو)

How to delete your spaceO account and associated personal data · Publisher: AYA DIGITAL LLC · App: com.spaceo.app

At AYA DIGITAL LLC, we uphold your absolute right to erasure, privacy self-determination, and data dignity. This page provides clear, compliant, and straightforward instructions for permanently deleting your spaceO (سبيس أو) account and wiping all associated personal records from our databases.

⚠️ What Happens When You Delete Your spaceO Account?

  • Immediate Profile Purge: Your full name, email address, handle/username, profile photo, bio, and digital passport badges are immediately wiped.
  • Instant Session Revocation: You will be immediately logged out of the app across all active mobile and web sessions, and authorization tokens are invalidated.
  • Retained Records (Statutory Only): Any past financial transaction records for paid memberships are stripped of personal identifiers and retained strictly in anonymized form to comply with mandatory tax, accounting, and anti-fraud regulations.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the spaceO app installed on your smartphone and are able to sign in:

  1. Open the spaceO app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings (الإعدادات ⚙️) icon in the navigation bar.
  3. Select "الخصوصية وإدارة الحساب (Privacy & Account Management)".
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap "حذف الحساب والبيانات نهائياً (Delete Account)".
  5. Type the confirmation word "DELETE" or "حذف" into the prompt and confirm.
  6. Result: Your profile, credentials, and sessions are immediately terminated and marked inactive.

Users who have uninstalled the app or cannot log in can request a complete manual database erasure by contacting our compliance desk:
