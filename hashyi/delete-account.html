  1. افتح تطبيق حاشي على جهاز iPhone أو Android الخاص بك.

  5. ضع علامة صح على الإقرار واضغط الزر الأحمر "حذف حسابي نهائياً".
  6. يتم مسح البيانات وتسجيل خروجك تلقائياً وبشكل فوري.

Account Deletion & Data Erasure — Hashyi

Mobile App: Hashyi (com.hashyi.app) · Developer & Operator: AyaDigital LLC · Last Updated: October 2026

At AyaDigital LLC, we uphold your fundamental right to personal data erasure and privacy self-determination. This dedicated page provides transparent instructions and mechanisms to permanently purge your account and personal profile data from the Hashyi (حاشي) platform.

⚠️ What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • Immediate Profile Wipe: Your full name, email address, mobile phone number, physical delivery address, and favorites list are permanently erased.
  • Session Revocation: All active authentication tokens and login sessions across all devices are immediately invalidated.
  • Anonymized Statutory Records: In full compliance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), statutory tax laws, and anti-fraud rules, historical financial invoice records are stripped of all personally identifiable markers and retained in strictly anonymized form.

Method 1: Direct In-App Self-Service Deletion (Fastest)

If you currently have the Hashyi app installed on your iPhone or Android device:

  1. Launch the Hashyi (حاشي) app on your device.
  2. Tap on Settings ⚙️ (الإعدادات) in the navigation bar.
  3. Select Legal (الشروط والخصوصية) and tap on Delete Account (حذف الحساب).
  4. Type DELETE into the confirmation field to confirm intent.
  5. Check the acknowledgement box and press the red "Permanently Delete My Account" button.
  6. Your account is immediately wiped and you will be signed out permanently.
