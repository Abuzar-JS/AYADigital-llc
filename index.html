Studio Privacy & Security Standards

Every mobile product published under AyaDigital LLC adheres strictly to our global data protection principles:

Data Minimization

We collect only the bare minimum user data strictly necessary to fulfill the application's core functionality (e.g., account profile, delivery coordination). No extraneous data is harvested.

Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers

Our applications do not sell your personal data, nor do we integrate cross-app advertising broker SDKs, behavioural trackers, or data-monetization networks.

Encryption in Transit & at Rest

All network communications between mobile clients and our backend infrastructure are secured via HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ encryption, backed by strict row-level database security.

Full Right to Erasure

In accordance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), Google Play Data Safety, GDPR, and Saudi PDPL, users maintain the right to delete their accounts and wipe personal data directly in-app or via web request.
