Account & Data Deletion — 5M Real Estate

Official instructions for permanent account and personal data deletion · Publisher: AyaDigital LLC · App: com.fivem.realestate

Your Right to Erasure

In strict adherence to Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), Google Play User Data policies, and GDPR / Saudi PDPL standards, users of 5M Real Estate (خمسة ملايين للعقارات) can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

⚠️ Data Categories Purged Upon Deletion:

  • User Profile Records: Registered name, phone number, and email address.
  • Real Estate Inquiries & Listings: Property inquiries, listing submissions, buy/sell/lease requests, and reverse wanted posts.
  • Session & Interaction Logs: Interactive smart assistant chat logs, search histories, and cached preferences.
  • Saved Metrics: Custom ROI calculations, saved listings, and user notification settings.

How to Request Deletion

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate Self-Service)

  1. Open the 5M Real Estate app on your device.
  2. Scroll down to the bottom footer of the main page.
  3. Tap the link labeled "Delete My Data" (حذف بياناتي).
  4. Complete the in-app confirmation modal and tap Submit.

Method 2: Direct Email Request (Web Method)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a direct web erasure request:

  • Official Email: tradea12027@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: 5M Real Estate Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Registered Phone]
  • Body Text: State your registered contact information and request permanent removal of your account and records.
